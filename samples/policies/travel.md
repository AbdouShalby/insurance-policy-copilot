# Cloud Kite Travel Insurance (fictional)

This is a wholly synthetic portfolio policy, not a real insurance product. Document ID: travel.

The travel policy covers trips up to 30 consecutive days. Emergency overseas medical expenses are covered up to USD 50000. The policy excludes professional sports and mountaineering above 4000 metres.

Baggage delay lasting more than 12 hours qualifies for reimbursement of essential purchases up to USD 200. Lost baggage has a maximum benefit of USD 1000 and requires a carrier loss report.

Trip cancellation due to a documented acute illness is covered up to USD 3000. Visa refusal is excluded. Claims must be submitted within 21 days after returning home, with original receipts and proof of travel.
