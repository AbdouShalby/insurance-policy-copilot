# Sandcastle Motor Insurance (fictional)

This is a wholly synthetic portfolio policy, not a real insurance product. Document ID: motor.

The motor policy covers accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle. The collision deductible is EGP 2000 for each claim. Drivers must be at least 25 years old and hold a valid driving licence.

Windscreen repairs are covered up to EGP 5000 per policy year without a deductible. Theft claims require a police report submitted within 48 hours of discovering the theft. Personal belongings inside the vehicle are excluded.

Roadside assistance includes towing up to 80 kilometres from the breakdown location. Flood damage is excluded unless the flood extension is listed on the schedule. The cancellation notice period is 14 calendar days.
