# Paper Lantern Medical Insurance (fictional)

This is a wholly synthetic portfolio policy, not a real insurance product. Document ID: medical.

The medical policy covers inpatient hospital treatment up to EGP 300000 per member per year. Planned hospital admissions require pre-authorisation at least 72 hours before admission. Emergency admission must be reported within 24 hours.

Outpatient consultations have a copayment of 20 percent. Dental treatment is limited to EGP 2500 per member per year. Cosmetic surgery is excluded unless medically necessary following an accident.

Pre-existing conditions have a waiting period of 180 days. Prescription medicines must be obtained from a network pharmacy. Maternity treatment has a waiting period of 10 months and a limit of EGP 20000.
