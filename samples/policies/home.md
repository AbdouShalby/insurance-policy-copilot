# Origami Harbour Home Insurance (fictional)

This is a wholly synthetic portfolio policy, not a real insurance product. Document ID: home.

The home policy covers fire damage to household contents up to EGP 400000. Water leakage from internal pipes is covered with a deductible of EGP 1500 per incident. Gradual damp and mould are excluded.

Alternative accommodation is reimbursed for up to 20 nights when a covered fire makes the home uninhabitable. Jewellery is limited to EGP 10000 in total unless individually listed on the schedule.

Unoccupied homes must be inspected every 14 days. Commercial stock stored at home is excluded. A contents inventory and photographs are required for a fire claim.
